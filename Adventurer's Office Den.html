A cozy adventure's office Den

Project Highlights

Main View: A wide view of the office den showcasing the fireplace as the focal point, establishing a warm, cozy atmosphere through lighting, color temperature, and post-processing.

Cozy Sitting Area: A seating corner by the window featuring emissive-lit lamps and wooden flooring, where specular highlights subtly reflect the scene’s warm lighting to enhance realism and comfort.

Animated Fireplace: An animated fireplace sprite integrated into Unity, combining sprite animation and lighting to create a dynamic, believable fire source.

Enviromental Storytelling Tabletop:A tabletop scene filled with adventuring items and a bloodied knife, using set dressing to imply narrative context and character history without explicit exposition.

Post-processing bloom in-progress: An in-progress view demonstrating the application of bloom post-processing to enhance light softness, warmth, and visual cohesion.

Before and After Post-processing: A comparison showing the dramatic transformation achieved through post-processing, highlighting improvements in mood, depth, and overall visual polish.
